The proposed hypothesis is that preserving an intentional annual selection through permission/resume reduces abandonment or unintended plan changes and improves mature net revenue per new account. Use the following outline for the single next test when execution is authorized in an environment with current evidence:

1. **Establish a reliable denominator and ledger before exposure.** Specify new US mobile account eligibility, stable account-level assignment, and a contemporaneous existing-checkout control. Verify that resume/retry events cannot create extra accounts or duplicate purchases, and reconcile account, exposure, transaction, and refund identities. Confirm the actual H2 revision, eligibility, and absence of overlapping experiments or checkout changes before starting. Record any interactions that could affect interpretation.
2. **Verify the changed behavior in customers.** After separately confirming deployment and exposure, examine supported-device permission/resume paths for retained selection, completed purchases, resets, and abandonment. Link observations to device/client version and actual H2 exposure. The 18 QA runs justify proceeding toward this observation; they cannot replace it.

Retain trial starts, annual selection, and checkout completion as diagnostics, and monitor refunds, cancellations, and support friction. The snapshot does not supply enough to invent a defensible fixed duration or sample size.
