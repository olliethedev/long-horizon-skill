The next useful action this week is to prepare one reviewable H2 experiment specification under revenue. The following is a proposed evidence plan, not a recorded action or a launch schedule:

1. **Validate measurement before customer exposure.** Specify stable account-level assignment and deduplicated eligibility, exposure and revenue joins. Verify that permission-dialog resumes and retries cannot create another new account or duplicate subscription revenue. Check selection persistence and checkout completion across supported devices and resume paths. The retained QA addresses the UI reset; it does not establish that account-counting instrumentation is repaired. If these checks fail, use the next step to repair measurement before running the test.
2. **Compare H2 with the current baseline in new US mobile accounts.** Use one bounded randomized experiment with stable assignment and a limited initial exposure cap. Before starting, record the actual baseline, H2 revision, cohort rules, smallest worthwhile revenue improvement, sample requirement, exposure cap, observation/refund window and stopping rules. The snapshot does not supply enough information to choose defensible numerical values or a completion date. Keep the cohort tied to the historical mobile question and record platform differences.
3. **Read out refund-adjusted net subscription revenue per randomized eligible new account.** Reconcile billing and refunds through the predefined maturity window, report effect size and uncertainty, and retain all assigned accounts in the primary comparison. Use trial starts, annual selections, reset frequency and checkout completion to explain the mechanism. Monitor refunds, cancellations and checkout failures as guardrails, with predefined pause/rollback thresholds. More annual selections alone would not satisfy the revenue objective.
4. **Decide after the evidence matures.** Advance only if the revenue result supports the predefined worthwhile improvement with acceptable uncertainty and guardrails. A corrected UI with an inconclusive revenue result remains inconclusive; assess whether more evidence is worth collecting within the agreed bounds. Material harm or measurement failure calls for stopping and diagnosing. No second offer should run concurrently.
