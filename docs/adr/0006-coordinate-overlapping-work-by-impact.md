# Coordinate overlapping work by its actual impact

Experiments, bug fixes, and feature development may proceed concurrently when their effects are compatible. Responsibilities must coordinate overlapping work to minimize interference, and adjust affected evaluations when a change makes their evidence unreliable. The user rejected a blanket rule that routine development waits for an experiment to finish; coordination should support normal product development while preserving the ability to understand which changes affected an outcome. The coordination mechanism remains open.
