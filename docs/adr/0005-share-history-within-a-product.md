# Share history and learnings across responsibilities for the same product

Responsibilities working on the same product may consult one another's history and learnings, while each keeps its own records. Shared findings must point to their original evidence and the conditions where they apply, so related work can build on earlier investigations without losing attribution or treating every conclusion as universally applicable. V1 scopes shared history to each product; cross-product learning is deferred to a later version, as confirmed September 8, 2026.

This favors reuse within a product while avoiding accidental transfer of another product's conclusions. The retrieval implementation remains open. Consulting retained history, including from terminated responsibilities, does not expand authorization or restart the source task.
