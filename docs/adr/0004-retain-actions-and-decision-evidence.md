# Retain action records and decision evidence until explicit deletion

The [simplified persistence decision](0016-persist-knowledge-tools-cannot-reconstruct.md) supersedes the broad retention scope below; this document records the earlier decision.

Keep action records and the evidence used to justify decisions during the responsibility's lifetime and after termination, until the owner explicitly deletes them. Maintain compact summaries for routine work while preserving searchable history for future responsibilities on the same project; terminating a task ends its future work and does not erase what it learned. Retaining underlying records accepts additional storage and retrieval work so omitted details, earlier failures, and later corrections remain recoverable months afterward.

Post-termination retention was explicitly confirmed September 8, 2026. This does not require keeping every raw tool response or conversation. [ADR 0013](0013-use-readable-files-for-durable-history.md) subsequently selects readable files with any index rebuildable from the original records. Consulting a terminated responsibility's history preserves source attribution and does not reactivate that task or transfer its authorization.
